# Zakkey

[English](#english) | [日本語](#日本語)

## English

I make mods and companion tools, mainly for racing games.

I have the ideas. I am still acquiring the technical skills—with some help from AI.

I build things, see whether they work, and fix them when they break.

> [!NOTE]
> **AI-assisted development**
>
> I actively use AI tools such as Codex and Cursor in my development workflow.
>
> If AI-assisted work is not your thing, feel free to hit the Back button. No hard feelings.
>
> I do not hide my use of AI, and I value testing and keeping clear records for anything I publish.

### How I work

- I am open about where I use AI
- I try to include source code, testing steps, and known limitations
- I treat “it launches” and “it works properly in gameplay” as two different things
- I do not redistribute original game data or copyrighted content belonging to others

Bug reports are welcome in English or Japanese. Reproducible steps and relevant logs are especially helpful.

### 制作方針

- AIの利用箇所を隠さない
- ソース、検証手順、既知の制限をできるだけ残す
- 「起動した」と「正常に遊べた」を分けて考える
- ゲーム本体のデータや権利者のコンテンツを再配布しない

不具合報告は日本語・英語のどちらでも歓迎します。再現手順と関連ログがあると助かります。
